My landlord wants me to vacate in 7 days without any written notice. Is that legal in Bangladesh?
J
Grounded in verified law
Generally, no. Under the tenancy law that applies to most rented premises, a landlord must give written notice before requiring a tenant to vacate, and a tenant cannot be evicted merely by an oral demand. The standard notice period is one month, not seven days.
  • A valid notice must be in writing and served properly.
  • Eviction without due process can be challenged in the relevant court.

The Penal Code, 1860 Ch. XVII — Offences against property §420

Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property

প্রতারণা এবং অসাধুভাবে সম্পত্তি অর্পণে প্ররোচিত করা

Act: XLV of 1860Section: 420Chapter: XVIIVerified
Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property to any person, or to make, alter or destroy the whole or any part of a valuable security, or anything which is signed or sealed, and which is capable of being converted into a valuable security, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.
In plain languageসহজ ভাষায়

If someone tricks you into handing over money, goods, or signing something valuable — that's an offence. The punishment can be up to seven years in prison plus a fine.

কেউ যদি প্রতারণা করে আপনাকে টাকা, জিনিস বা মূল্যবান কিছুতে সই করতে বাধ্য করে — তা অপরাধ। শাস্তি সাত বছর পর্যন্ত কারাদণ্ড ও জরিমানা।

Exampleউদাহরণ

A seller takes advance payment online for a phone, promising delivery, but never intended to send it and disappears. That can amount to cheating under §420.

একজন বিক্রেতা অনলাইনে ফোনের অগ্রিম টাকা নিয়ে ডেলিভারির প্রতিশ্রুতি দেয়, কিন্তু পাঠানোর ইচ্ছাই ছিল না — এটি §৪২০ অনুযায়ী প্রতারণা হতে পারে।
